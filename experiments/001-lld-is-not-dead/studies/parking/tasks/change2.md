Add monthly passes and a fee cap to the parking lot.

- New command `pass <plate>` registers a monthly pass and prints `PASS <plate>`. Registering the same plate again is harmless and prints the same line. A pass can be registered before or after the vehicle parks, and stays valid for later visits.
- No stay is charged for more than 8 hours: a 10-hour stay costs the same as an 8-hour stay.
- Vehicles with a pass pay half the fee, applied after the cap.
- These rules apply to every fee the lot reports.
- Everything else behaves exactly as before. Keep the README up to date.
