Add electric vehicles to the parking lot.

- New vehicle type `ev`: `park <plate> ev`.
- The lot gets two new charging spots, `C1` and `C2`.
- An EV takes a free charging spot first (C1 before C2). If both are taken, it parks wherever a car would.
- Other vehicles never use charging spots.
- EVs pay the car rate (20/hour), plus a 10/hour charging fee while in a charging spot.
- Everything else behaves exactly as before. Keep the README up to date.
